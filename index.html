Draw in the air with your finger

  1. Allow the camera and microphone when Chrome asks.
  2. Hold up one hand. A ring follows your index fingertip.
  3. Say “draw” to put the pen down, then move your finger.
  4. Say “stop” to lift the pen. Move somewhere else and say “draw” again.
Voice commands
draw · go · start Pen down
stop · up Pen up
undo · clear Remove last line · erase everything
red · blue · green · black · white Pen color
thicker · thinner Pen width

No mic or a noisy room? Switch to Pinch (top right): touch your thumb and index finger together to draw, open them to stop.

Keyboard: Space = draw/stop, Z = undo, C = clear. Voice needs Chrome or Edge and an internet connection. Your drawing is saved in this browser.
