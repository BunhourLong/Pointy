Draw in the air with your finger

  1. Allow the camera and microphone when the browser asks.
  2. Hold up one hand. A ring follows your index fingertip.
  3. Say “draw” to put the pen down, then move your finger.
  4. Say “stop” to lift the pen. Move somewhere else and say “draw” again.
Hand gestures (any mode)
✊ Fist Eraser: rub over lines to erase them
👌 Pinch Thumb on index, other fingers open: grab a drawing and move it. Let go over the 🗑️ corner to delete it.
Voice commands
draw · go · start · down Pen down
stop · up · end · done Pen up
undo · clear Remove last line · erase everything
red · blue · green · black · white Pen color
thicker · thinner Pen width

No mic or a noisy room? Switch to Hand (top right): ☝️ point with just your index finger to draw, 🖐 open your hand to stop.

The status bar (top left) shows what the mic heard. Keyboard: Space = draw/stop, Z = undo, C = clear. Voice runs offline in your browser; the first visit downloads a ~40 MB voice model. Your drawing is saved in this browser.
